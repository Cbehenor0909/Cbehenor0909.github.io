About RAVEL

Robotics · Analysis · Visualization · Embodiment · Learning

Purpose and scientific scope

RAVEL is a personal research and teaching platform, intended to grow throughout a PhD and beyond. It connects physical intuition, mathematical representation, visual exploration, and eventually measurement.

Its scope spans mechanics, dynamics, control, sensing, human movement, assistive systems, and robotics. The four domains offer different perspectives on connected ideas.

How the content is organized

A module begins with one question and an explicit model. A note gives room to work through an explanation. A path connects small concepts into a teaching sequence. Scientific assumptions belong beside the visual, where they can be examined.

About the creator

RAVEL is developed as a personal PhD research and teaching project. A named author biography and affiliation have not yet been added.

Using the collection

Use an available module as a starting point for a mentoring conversation, a concept review, or a teaching demonstration. Check its status and assumptions before relying on it. “Functional” describes working software; it does not mean scientifically reviewed or teaching-tested.

The collection currently contains two working explorations. Planned entries describe future directions, and all current examples are synthetic.
