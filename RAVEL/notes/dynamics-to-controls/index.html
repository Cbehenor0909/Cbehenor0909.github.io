Practice crossing the boundary

  • What changes if position is not measured? Rebuild CCC and any direct-feedthrough term, then test observability before discussing an observer.
  • What changes if a constant force disturbance is added? Calculate the resulting equilibrium error; the nominal reference prefilter does not remove every disturbance.
  • Why can energy and asymptotic stability differ? Undamped oscillation conserves mechanical energy, while viscous damping dissipates energy and produces decay in this positive-stiffness model.
