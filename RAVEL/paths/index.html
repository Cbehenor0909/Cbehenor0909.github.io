Learning Paths

Follow an ordered sequence of readings and interactive models, from a scientific question to its application.

12 steps

Human Sensing → Active Robotics Control ↗

Follow human motion through physical representation, sensing, estimation and feedback to robot action and interaction.

6 steps

Human Sensing Foundations ↗

From raw human-motion signals to calibrated, time-aligned segment and state estimates.

7 steps

Active Robotics Control ↗

Build the chain from robot configuration and force mapping to sampled feedback and compliant interaction.

6 steps

Human Balance Foundations ↗

From the ground beneath us to the mechanics of staying upright.

10 steps

Dynamics Qualifying Exam Study Path ↗

Build oral-exam explanations from vector language through kinematics, inertia, forces, energy and equations of motion.

9 steps

Controls Qualifying Exam Study Path ↗

Describe one dynamical system in time, state space, Laplace and frequency domains, then design and check feedback.
