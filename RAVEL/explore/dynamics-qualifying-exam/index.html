Dynamics Qualifying Exam

Nine concept groups and 37 outline topics, connected to derivations, worked examples and rotating-frame explorations.

The supplied Analytical Dynamics Oral Qualifying Exam Concepts outline, dated October 16, 2024, organizes its fundamental topics into nine groups. The map below preserves all 37 topic entries and connects each to a RAVEL lesson. Several topics share one lesson because their derivations use the same physical ideas.

Follow the Dynamics study path for a teaching order, or choose a topic directly. Reading lessons contain assumptions, derivations, worked calculations and oral questions; the turntable, spatial chain and COM lessons also provide interactive models. All explanations and practice problems are authored for this site.

Build a problem from fundamentals

Start with a system boundary and a frame. Define coordinates and constraints. Compute kinematics before inserting forces, then choose force/momentum or energy equations. Finish with units, limiting cases and an independent check. A diagram’s viewing angle does not establish its physical reference frame.

Connect to controls

A mechanical equation becomes a control model only after inputs, outputs and states are declared. Continue to Controls and the mechanics-to-controls example to connect a mass-spring-damper equation to state space, a transfer function and feedback.
