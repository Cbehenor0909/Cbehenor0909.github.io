Active Robotics Control

Turn references and estimates into robot motion and interaction forces using kinematics, dynamics and sampled feedback.

An active robot needs more than a desired pose. It needs a consistent configuration, an actuator model, an estimated state and a controller whose timing and force limits match its assumptions.

Use the existing Controls lessons for loop analysis, then examine how robot geometry, contact and human inputs change the interpretation. These available lessons and synthetic models establish concepts; they do not claim a working hardware controller.
