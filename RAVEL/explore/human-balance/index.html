Human Balance

Connect ground contact, body motion, and the actions that keep movement possible.

Start with the geometry of contact. Then ask what changes once the body is moving, forces act, and new steps become possible.

Every concept now connects to an available lesson. Begin with contact geometry and mass distribution, distinguish COM from force-derived COP, then add velocity, support transitions and recovery actions. Dynamic-balance predictions depend on the stated inverted-pendulum assumptions.

Connect these mechanics to Human Sensing when asking how motion and contact are measured, and to Active Robotics Control when asking how inputs change the motion.
